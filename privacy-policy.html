MyFoodBill – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. Who we are

MyFoodBill is an app that lets customers order and pay at restaurants, hotels, nashta centres, dhabas and similar places (each called a "Food Place"), and lets Food Place owners manage their menu, orders and bills. This policy explains what information the app keeps, why, and the choices you have. You can write to us at myfoodbill20@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

When you register: your name, mobile number, a password or PIN, a recovery code that you choose, and an email address if you wish to add one (the email is optional). Your password and recovery code are stored in a scrambled form that nobody, including us, can read.

When you use the app: your orders (items, amounts, table or parcel, payment method, dates and times); for parcel delivery, the delivery addresses you choose to save; your comments if you send us feedback.

If you are a Food Place owner: your Food Place name, address, contact number, UPI ID, menu, tables, charges, hours and the orders and sales your customers place.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or files. The camera is used only to scan a table QR code, and the picture is not stored. We do not show advertisements and we do not sell your information.

3. Why we use it

To create your account and log you in; to send your order to the Food Place and show you its progress; to show Food Place owners the orders, bills and summaries they need; to email you a verification or password-reset code if you added an email, and to let you reset a forgotten password with your recovery code; and to improve the app using the feedback you send.

4. Who can see it

Customers: the Food Place you order from sees your name, mobile number, order details and, for delivery, the address you chose. Other customers see nothing about you.

Owners: your customers see your Food Place name, address, contact number, menu and UPI ID.

Only the MyFoodBill team can read feedback you send privately.

We share information only with the services that run the app for us: Supabase (database and login, hosting your data) and our email provider (sending codes). They may process it only to provide those services. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

5. Payments

The app does not hold your money. When you pay by UPI, the money goes directly to the Food Place's UPI account through your own UPI app. Cash and card payments are handled by the Food Place. We store only that a payment was made and how, not your card or bank details.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. Orders are kept so that Food Places have their sales records and tax summaries. When you delete your account the information listed below is removed.

7. Deleting your account and your data

You can delete your account any time in the app: Settings > Delete my account. This removes your login, profile, saved addresses and feedback.

Customers: your past orders stay in the Food Place's records for their accounts, but your name, mobile number and delivery address are removed from them.

Owners: your Food Place, menu, tables, charges and all its orders are deleted permanently. Download or print any summaries you need first.

If you cannot use the app, write to myfoodbill20@gmail.com with your registered mobile number (and from your registered email, if you added one). We will check that it is you and delete the account for you.

8. Security

Information travels over encrypted connections and is protected by access rules, so a customer can see only his own orders and an owner only his own Food Place. No system is perfectly safe, so please keep your password or PIN private.

9. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent, by using Settings in the app or by writing to myfoodbill20@gmail.com. We follow the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 of India.

10. Children

MyFoodBill is meant for people aged 18 and over. Please do not register if you are younger.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update it in the app with a new date.
